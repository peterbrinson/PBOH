**Developer:** Croteam | **Year:** 2014 | **Platform:** PC, console
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/257510/The_Talos_Principle/)**

A first-person philosophical puzzle game. Its variation on keys-and-doors: a single kind of key opens three different kinds of doors. The purple portal visible in the first moments serves as doorway, landmark, and objective — a clean example of [[Reusing Objects and Spaces]].
