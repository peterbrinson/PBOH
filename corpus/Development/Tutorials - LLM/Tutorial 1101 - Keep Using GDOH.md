## 0. Introduction

**Outcome.** You will know the three main ways to work with GDOH, where it saves your files, and how to resume later.

This page follows any quickstart. It is not another setup requirement.

---

## 1. Three Kinds of Conversation

### Plan a project

Describe an idea for The Investigator, The Traveler, The Dreamer, or Bounded Worlds. GDOH will help shape it and, when the idea is ready, produce a Prototype Plan.

### Get tutorial help

Name the tutorial and the place where you are stuck. Include what you expected, what happened instead, and any values or messages you can see.

### Ask a concept question

Ask about a Blueprint node, Unreal concept, design framework, or example from the course corpus. GDOH will tell you whether its answer comes from the course materials or from general knowledge.

---

## 2. Know Where Your Files Go

GDOH saves personal work inside `student-notes-private/`:

- `projects/` — Prototype Plans
- `notes/` — ideas, fixes, and discoveries you ask it to remember
- `references/` — games, films, or books you save as examples
- `instructor-questions.md` — questions to bring to class or office hours
- `where-we-left-off.md` — a short brief for the next session

These files belong to you. GDOH confirms the location whenever it saves something.

---

## 3. Resume Later

Open the same `GDOH` folder in the same tool and type:

```
Start a GET session.
```

If `student-notes-private/where-we-left-off.md` contains a recent brief, GDOH will offer to continue from it.
