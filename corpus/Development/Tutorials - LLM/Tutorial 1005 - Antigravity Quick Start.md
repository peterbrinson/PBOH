## 0. Introduction

**Outcome.** By the end of this tutorial you have The GET on your computer, open in the Gemini desktop app (Antigravity), and you've held your first GET conversation — brought an idea, talked it through, and saved the plan it gave you.

This is the everything-on-one-page path. **No GitHub account, no git, no terminal.** You need three things:

- A laptop (Windows or Mac)
- A **Google account** (free)

**Before you start.** The GET thinks about your idea through the Situated Player Roles — **The Investigator** and **The Traveler** — and the **Bounded Worlds** framework. You've studied these in class. If you want to refresh, the pages are on the class site: [[The-GET/corpus/Design/Storytelling/index|Situated Player Roles]] and [[The-GET/corpus/Design/Worldbuilding/index|Bounded Worlds]].

---

## 1. Download The GET

*The GET is a folder of files. You'll download it as a ZIP straight from the project page — no account needed.*

1. In your browser, go to **[github.com/vLabUSC/The-GET](https://github.com/vLabUSC/The-GET)**.
2. Click the green **Code** button (top-right of the file list).
3. Click **Download ZIP**.
4. Unzip it:
   - **Windows:** right-click the downloaded file → **Extract All…**
   - **macOS:** double-click the downloaded file
5. You now have a folder named **`The-GET-main`**. Move it somewhere you can find again — your **Documents** folder is good.
6. Rename the folder from `The-GET-main` to **`The-GET`**.

<span style="color:#cb5d21">**Don't miss this step:**</span> after unzipping, open the folder and check what's inside. You should see folders named `agent/` and `corpus/` directly inside. If instead you see *another* folder called `The-GET-main`, that inner folder is the real one — use it.

---

## 2. Install Antigravity

*Antigravity is the Gemini desktop app — the program you'll talk to The GET through.*

1. Download **Antigravity** for your platform and install it.
2. Open it and **sign in with your Google account**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Not the Antigravity API.** Make sure you are using the standard Antigravity desktop app, and not the more advanced Antigravity API. The API involves complexities (like setting up developer keys and custom tooling) that are more advanced than we need.

---

## 3. Open Your GET Folder

The essential part of this setup is making sure Antigravity can see your GET folder.

1. In the Antigravity UI, look for workspace or folder settings (**Open Folder**).
2. Point it directly at the **`The-GET`** folder you made in Chapter 1.

---

## 4. Pick the Model

Once the app is running, select an appropriate model. We recommend **Gemini 3.1 Pro (High)** for the best results.

---

## 5. Start a GET Session

In the chat panel, type exactly:

```
Start a GET session.
```

The GET will greet you and ask what you're working on. It knows the roles you've learned — **The Investigator**, **The Traveler** — and the **Bounded Worlds** framework. Any of these is a fine place to start.

---

## 6. Bring Your Idea

Tell The GET about an idea you have. Something concrete is best — describe the **experience**, more than the mechanics.

You don't need a finished concept. A situation you can picture is enough — perhaps 4 to 8 sentences. It can be half-baked. There can be unknowns.

> [!NOTE]
> You can write to The GET in Japanese if you prefer. Its pages are in English, but the conversation can happen in your language.

---

## 7. Save What You Get

After some back and forth, The GET will draft a plan — you'll know it when you see it (it includes concrete sections, and might include a "Build Order"). Two things:

- Ask it to **save the plan to a file** in the folder — for example: *"save this plan to project-plan.md"*.
- If your instructor asks for it, also **copy the whole conversation** into a text file. The conversation itself is the most useful thing to see.

---

## Troubleshooting

### It acts like a normal chatbot, not The GET

Antigravity is probably not looking at the right folder. Check that the folder you opened in Chapter 3 is the one containing `agent/` and `corpus/`, then type `Start a GET session.` again.

### I can't find the folder I unzipped

Check your **Downloads** folder — unzipped folders land next to the ZIP file. Move the folder to Documents and continue from Chapter 1, step 5.

### The sign-in doesn't work

Antigravity needs a Google account. If you're signed into several, pick your personal one.
