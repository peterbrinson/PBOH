Game Development Principles — lecture pages for USC's CTIN 389, converted from the course slide decks. (A few pages have fuller image sets than others; image coverage is still being filled in.)

- [[Week 1.2 - Lost Woods]] — directing player attention; designing to make a player feel lost, then found.
- [[Week 2.1 - Keys and Doors]] — how games gate progress, from literal keys to earned abilities, and teaching the player to play.
- [[Week 3.2 - Workspaces and Splines]] — setting up Perforce workspaces, and an introduction to splines in Unity.
- [[Week 4.1 - Animation and Assets]] — importing and driving character animations, and a field guide to finding game assets.
- [[Week 5.1 - Quandaries]] — goals, rewards, and designing interesting choices.
- [[Week 8.1 - Unreal Tutorial]] — a tour of the Unreal Editor and Blueprints for Unity-trained designers.
- [[Week 8.2 - Subtext and Theme]] — meaning through metaphor, from Alice in Wonderland to surrealism to *Papo y Yo*.
- [[Week 9.2 - Mechanics and Emotion]] — the self-conscious emotions games are uniquely able to evoke.
- [[Week 10.1 - Technology and Development]] — a developer's ever-changing toolset, and why adaptability is the real skill.
- [[Week 12.2 - Evoking Emotion]] — a recap of the four arcs of experience, applied across a wide set of games.
