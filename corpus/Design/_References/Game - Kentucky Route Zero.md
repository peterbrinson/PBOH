## What It Is
A magical-realist adventure in five acts: a truck driver making one last delivery searches for the Zero, a secret highway beneath Kentucky, gathering a caravan of debt-haunted strangers. Scenes are passed *through* rather than solved — dialogue choices shape tone and memory, not outcomes — and the world (a distillery staffed by skeletons, a museum of dwellings, an underground river) runs on dream logic grounded in real economic grief. The reference for atmosphere-first, theatrical staging in games.
