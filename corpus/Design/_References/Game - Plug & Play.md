## What It Is
A 15-minute interactive animation in stark black-and-white: figures with plugs and sockets for heads connect, disconnect, obey and defy a commanding voice, and reach for each other. Based on Frei's award-winning animated short, it sits deliberately on the line between game and film — minimal forms, absurdist logic, and a surprising amount of feeling about connection and consent. (Same maker as Time Flies and KIDS.)
