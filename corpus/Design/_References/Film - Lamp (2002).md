**Director:** Tom Kuntz, Mike Maguire (MJZ) | **Year:** 2002 | **Runtime:** 1 min

A short film (originally an IKEA commercial directed by Spike Jonze, but the 2002 *Lamp* short referenced in lecture is the standalone version of similar form) about a discarded lamp left out in the rain. Its emotional toolkit: cinematography, color, low POV, anthropomorphization, music — and a twist ending that highlights how absurd the manufactured sympathy was. A reference for [[Designed Experiences]].
