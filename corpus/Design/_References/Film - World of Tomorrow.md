## What It Is
A 4-year-old named Emily is visited by her adult third-generation clone from the distant future, who takes her on a guided tour through time — memory banks, digital afterlives, dying stars, and a moment when love was copied and stored. Each stop is a minimal, bounded world built from a single memory or idea; the clone narrates with clinical detachment while Emily's improvised non-sequiturs run underneath. Crayon drawings and flat color carry the full weight of mortality. The reference for the tour structure (guided, passive, revelatory), worlds-as-memories, and devastating minimalism — maximum feeling from almost nothing drawn.
