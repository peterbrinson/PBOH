**Developer:** BioWare | **Year:** 2003 | **Platform:** PC, console
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/32370/STAR_WARS_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic/)**

A Star Wars CRPG built around a light-side / dark-side alignment meter. Every dialogue and action choice nudges the bar in one direction, and that aggregate determines what the world looks like and how it ends. A clean example of procedural rhetoric: the game has a strong implicit theory about morality as a one-dimensional axis.
