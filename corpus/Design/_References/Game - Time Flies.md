## What It Is
You are a fly. Your lifespan matches the life expectancy of the country you set — a few real minutes — and you have a bucket list: learn an instrument, read a book, get rich, make someone smile. Each tiny life is spent bumbling through hand-drawn rooms trying to check items off before you drop. Mortality as a mechanic at its lightest possible touch: the timer is the whole design, and it's funny until it isn't. (Same maker as Plug & Play.)
