## What It Is
You are an octopus in a suit passing as a suburban father, and the controls are deliberately unruly — each flailing limb steered separately through mundane tasks like mowing the lawn and grocery shopping, without arousing suspicion. The comedy is the body, but the heart is the premise: a game about hiding what you are from the people you love, told entirely through how hard it is to walk.
