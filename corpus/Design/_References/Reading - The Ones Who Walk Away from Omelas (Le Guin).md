**Author:** Ursula K. Le Guin · **Year:** 1973

A short story set in Omelas, a city of extraordinary happiness and beauty. The story's central question: the city's prosperity depends on the suffering of a single child, kept alone in a basement. Everyone knows. Some walk away.

Le Guin calls Omelas a "psychomyth" — a myth about your own ethical imagination.

## Relevance to Worldbuilding

Omelas is a world defined entirely by its central dilemma. There is almost no plot, but it provides clear themes of fairness, freedom, and complacency.
