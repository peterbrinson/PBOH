## What It Is
A paranoid man tries to get home to his mother, and the world escalates around him in exact proportion to his anxiety — the street outside his apartment is a war zone, strangers' kindness curdles into captivity, an animated play swallows decades of his life. The environment isn't *distorted by* his fear; it **is** his fear, staged literally. A maximalist example of a world built from one character's interior state.
