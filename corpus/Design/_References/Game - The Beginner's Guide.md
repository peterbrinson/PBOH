**Developer:** Davey Wreden | **Year:** 2015 | **Platform:** PC
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/303210/The_Beginners_Guide/)**

A first-person narrative game framed as a guided tour through another (fictional) designer's body of work. The sense that something is wrong builds slowly through the second half — the player anticipates a change without knowing what shape it will take.
