Poetry collection by Franny Choi (2022). [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/World-Keeps-Ending-Goes/dp/0063240084)

## Relevance to Worldbuilding
Choi's poems sit at the intersection of apocalypse, survival, and continuity — worlds that end and reconstitute themselves. The collection asks how people carry entire worlds inside them, and what it means to persist when the ground keeps shifting. Useful framing for thinking about worldbuilding not as architecture but as ongoing, embodied experience.
