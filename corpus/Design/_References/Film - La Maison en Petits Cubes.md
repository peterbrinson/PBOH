## What It Is
An old widower lives in a town slowly drowning in water; each time the level rises, he builds a new cube of a room on top of his house. When he drops his pipe through the floor hatch, he puts on a diving suit and descends — down through every submerged story of his own home, each level holding the memories of the life he lived there. The house is a vertical archive of a whole life, read from the most recent layer to the oldest.
