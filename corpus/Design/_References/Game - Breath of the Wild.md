## What It Is
An open-world action-adventure set in a ruined Hyrule a century after Calamity Ganon's destruction. Link wakes from a hundred-year sleep with no equipment and no map markers, free to approach the world's challenges in any order. Shrines scattered across the landscape are self-contained puzzle dungeons solved with a small set of physics-based runes — Magnesis (move metal), Stasis (freeze time on objects), Cryonis (raise ice pillars from water), and remote bombs — that recombine across the open world.
