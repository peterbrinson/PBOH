## What It Is
You hold up a photograph — a postcard, a sketch, a child's drawing — and place it into the world, where it becomes real, walkable geometry that *replaces* whatever was behind it. Bridges from postcards, doors from sketches, whole rooms cut out of pictures. One reality-bending camera mechanic, taught and escalated with total clarity. The reference for perspective-as-mechanic and for a single impossible rule explored to exhaustion.
