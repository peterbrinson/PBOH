**Game:** *The Exit 8* — Kotake Create, 2023 (PC, Switch) | **Film:** *Exit 8* — dir. Genki Kawamura, 2025

A tiny [[World - Backrooms|liminal]] world: an endless looping underground passageway — a Japanese subway corridor — that you can only escape by reading the space correctly.

## The Rule Is the World

The world states its own rules on a sign, and the whole game is obeying them:

> - Don't overlook any anomalies.
> - If you find an anomaly, turn back immediately.
> - If you don't find an anomaly, don't turn back.
> - Go to Exit 8.

You walk the same corridor again and again. Each loop, something may have changed — a poster, a light, a stain, the man walking toward you. Spot the difference and turn back; if nothing's wrong, walk on. Get it right and the exit number ticks up — 0, 1, 2 … to **Exit 8** and freedom. Get it wrong and you're back at 0.

This is [[Teaching the Rules of the World|teaching the rules of the world]] at its most literal — the world hands you its instructions — and a world built outward from a simple core: attention and memory.

## A Looping Closed World

In [[Bounded Worlds]] Exit 8 sits at the **Closed** edge with a Threshold goal: you feel the boundary every loop  until you learn to read the space. Like *P.T.*, the dread comes from a familiar place that should lead somewhere and instead returns you to its own start.

It shares the [[World - Backrooms|Backrooms']] liminal-space DNA — fluorescent, anonymous, semi-public architecture emptied of purpose — but where the Backrooms is infinite and authorless, Exit 8 is a single authored loop with a precise rule. Both turn an ordinary transitional space into a trap.

## Game → Film

The 2023 game (by studio Kotake Create) became a phenomenon for its purity. In 2025 it became a live-action film — *Exit 8*, directed by Genki Kawamura, starring Kazunari Ninomiya — which premiered at the Cannes Midnight Screenings (the first live-action video-game adaptation to screen there) and went on to become one of the best-reviewed game-to-film adaptations.
