## What It Is
A celebrated Mexican journalist-filmmaker drifts through a limbo built from his own memory and self-doubt — scenes swell into dream sequences (a dance floor, a desert of bodies, a flooded apartment) and bleed into one another without boundaries. Autobiography staged as dream logic; the title's "bardo" is the Buddhist state between death and rebirth, and the film's spaces behave accordingly.
