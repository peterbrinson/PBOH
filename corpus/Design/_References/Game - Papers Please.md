## What It Is
A border-checkpoint simulator set in the fictional communist state of Arstotzka. The player is an immigration inspector processing travelers one at a time — comparing passports, work permits, entry tickets, and ID cards against an ever-changing rulebook. Approve or deny. Catch a forged seal or expired date and you're paid; miss one and your family doesn't eat. The bureaucratic procedure *is* the gameplay, and moral pressure builds as the rules grow more punitive.
