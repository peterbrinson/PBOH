## What It Is

A moody puzzle game disguised as a cheerful, broken arcade game. The player begins by trying to play the game presented on screen, then discovers that its menus, errors, code, and apparent machine are all part of the playable world.

## Expectation and Payoff

The title, menu, graphics, and first simple actions establish the contract of a crude pony-themed arcade game. Small malfunctions make that contract unstable before the game reveals a second layer behind the interface.

The surprise is not a single unrelated twist. Each failure of the arcade game teaches the player to treat the interface itself as a space to investigate and manipulate. The payoff changes what kind of game the player believes they are playing.
