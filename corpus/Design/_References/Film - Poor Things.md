## What It Is
A woman revived with a new, unformed mind tours a heightened, candy-colored not-quite-Victorian world — Lisbon, a cruise ship, Paris — encountering everything (food, sex, cruelty, philosophy) for the first time, without shame or prior script. The journey structure is the point: each place is a stage of a mind assembling itself, and the stylized world reads as *her* expanding perception.
