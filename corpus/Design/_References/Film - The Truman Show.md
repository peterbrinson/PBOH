## What It Is
A man unknowingly lives his whole life inside a colossal soundstage — his town, his friends, his weather all produced for a broadcast he's never seen. The world is built specifically to keep one person from leaving it: travel agencies sell fear, roads loop back, and the sea hides the wall. The ending — a sailboat bumping into a painted sky, a staircase, an exit door — is the definitive image of a bounded world and its edge.
