## What It Is
Two brothers journey to fetch a cure for their dying father — and one player controls both at once, one brother per thumbstick. Every puzzle is built on coordinating the pair (the elder boosts, the younger squeezes through bars), so the control scheme quietly becomes the relationship. The famous final beat works *because* of the controls, not despite them — the single strongest example of a control scheme as the story's meaning. The 2024 remake adds two-player co-op, one brother each.
