## What It Is
A suburban housewife develops a mysterious illness — perfume, exhaust, her own living room seem to be poisoning her — and the film never confirms whether the cause is environmental or psychological. Shot with clinical wide framing that keeps her small inside immaculate interiors, it builds dread entirely from *tone*: nothing jumps out, nothing is explained, and the ordinary world simply stops feeling safe. A master class in sustained, sourceless unease.
