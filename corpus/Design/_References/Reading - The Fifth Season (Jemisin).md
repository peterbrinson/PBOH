Novel by N.K. Jemisin (2015). First book of the Broken Earth trilogy. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Fifth-Season-Broken-Earth/dp/0316229296)

## Relevance to Worldbuilding
A world built on catastrophe. The Stillness is a single supercontinent wracked by recurring apocalyptic seismic events called Fifth Seasons — civilization-ending disasters that happen often enough that society has organized entirely around surviving them.

Orogenes — people who can sense and manipulate seismic energy — are feared, controlled, and enslaved by the ruling system despite being the only ones who can stop earthquakes. The novel braids three timelines.
