## What It Is
A first-person descent through a gigantic, abstract megastructure — endless concrete halls, shafts of light, geometry that shifts as you pass — with no story, no guidance, and no explanation of what the structure is. The design goal, in the developer's words, is to make the player feel lost and small inside an unknown universe, and marveled by it. The reference for monumental architecture as the entire experience.
