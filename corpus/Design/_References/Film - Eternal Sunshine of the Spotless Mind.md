## What It Is
A man undergoes a procedure to erase his memories of a failed relationship — and midway through, changes his mind. Most of the film takes place *inside the memories as they're being deleted*: he flees with the remembered version of her through collapsing locations, hiding her in memories where she doesn't belong. Memory as a traversable, crumbling space — rooms losing their walls, faces blurring, a beach house disintegrating around a conversation.
