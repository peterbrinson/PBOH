## What It Is
A narrative exploration game that unfolds room by room through the Finch family house, each room belonging to a different member of the family. As Edith explores, an object in each room — a letter, a diary, a flipbook — opens into a short playable story told from that relative's perspective, usually the story of how they died. Each one has its own visual style and its own way of playing. You live one relative's story at a time and gradually piece together the family's larger history.

## As a Bounded World
A **Threshold** world (see [[Bounded Worlds]]). The house is the world you return to; each object is a doorway out of it. You cross into a relative's story, play it under its own rules, and come back to the house — then find the next door. The crossing repeats, room by room, and every return leaves you knowing the family a little better.
