**Developer:** Mats Borges (USC undergraduate) | **Year:** 2020 | **Platform:** PC

A student project that presents as a text-based multiple choice game written in Twine. After a few minutes, the text interface slowly fades out to reveal the interior of a 3D room — and the player realizes they can walk out the door into an expansive landscape. A small-scale example of revelation reframing a player's understanding of what game they've been playing.
