**Fame tier:** famous (the canonical expressionist film)
**→ [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cabinet_of_Dr._Caligari)**

## What It Is
A hypnotist uses a sleepwalker to commit murders — told through sets that are openly *wrong*: painted shadows, leaning walls, streets that twist at impossible angles. The world itself is warped to match a narrator's madness, and the frame story (an early use of the device) reveals the whole tale as a delusion, recoloring everything seen. The original proof that a world's *look* can be the inside of a mind.
