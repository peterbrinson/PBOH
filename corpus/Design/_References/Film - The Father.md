## What It Is
Dementia rendered from the *inside*. An elderly man's apartment quietly rearranges itself between scenes — furniture moves, rooms merge with his daughter's flat, the same conversation replays with different faces in the same roles. The audience is never given a stable reality to stand on; the film's spatial continuity errors *are* the condition. The strongest example of environment-as-unreliable-narrator.
