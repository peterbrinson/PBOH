## What It Is
A small beetle carries entire worlds as glowing orbs on its back — and can dive *into* any orb, or carry one orb inside another. Every puzzle is built from that single nested-worlds rule, taught wordlessly and escalated with immaculate clarity until you're juggling realities like keys. The reference for worlds-within-worlds as a playable structure rather than a story conceit.
