## What It Is
A girl's mind rendered as a literal, navigable world: emotions as a control-room crew, memories as glowing orbs in vast shelved canyons, personality as islands that can crumble, a train of thought you can ride. The most thorough mainstream example of building an *interior* as physical architecture — every space is a metaphor that also works as a place.
