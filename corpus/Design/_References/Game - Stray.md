## What It Is
A stray cat falls into a sealed, neon-lit underground city populated entirely by robots who have inherited human culture, and works its way back toward the surface with a small drone translator. The whole design flows from the body: you move through a world built for other inhabitants — rooftops, pipes, gaps — as the one creature the architecture wasn't made for. Bounded world, found companionship, and cat-verbs (knock things off shelves, scratch, curl up) as world-reading tools.
