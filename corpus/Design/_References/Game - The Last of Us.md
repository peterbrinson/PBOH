**Developer:** Naughty Dog | **Year:** 2013 (remake 2022) | **Platform:** PC, PlayStation
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1888930/The_Last_of_Us_Part_I/)**

A third-person post-apocalyptic action-adventure. Many of its key-and-door puzzles use environmental fiction: a powered door needs a generator turned on; a high jump needs a dumpster pushed into position. The keys are tools and props, not literal keys.
