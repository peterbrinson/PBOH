## What It Is
An English village, golden-hour and utterly empty, in the minutes after an apocalypse no one witnessed. The vanished residents persist as drifting traces of light that replay fragments of their last conversations — you wander lanes, pubs, and farms, tuning into these echoes to piece together what came for the village and what it meant to each person. BAFTA-winning score. The reference for reconstructing a *community* (not just a household) from an emptied space.
