## What It Is
A porter carries cargo across a depopulated, post-apocalyptic America on foot — and the *walking itself* is the game: weight distribution, balance on scree, river crossings, rope and ladder placement. Other players' structures appear in your world (bridges, paths, warnings), so the land slowly fills with anonymous mutual aid. The definitive case that traversal — terrain, weight, exhaustion, route-planning — can carry an entire AAA game.
