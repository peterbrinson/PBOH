**Developer:** Team Ico | **Year:** 2005 (remake 2018) | **Platform:** PS2, PS3, PS4
**→ [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP9000-CUSA08010_00-SHADOWOFCOLOSSUS)**

A third-person action game in which the player must kill sixteen massive creatures to revive a girl. Early colossi feel like ARPG bosses: exciting, satisfying. Over time the satisfaction sours — the colossi aren't monstrous but majestic, and the player isn't a hero but a villain. A reference for how mechanical and aesthetic framing can carry a moral argument.
