A.K. Dewdney, *The Planiverse: Computer Contact with a Two-Dimensional World* (1984)

## What It Is

A computer scientist makes contact with a creature named Yendred living in a strictly two-dimensional universe. The book traces what life, physics, biology, architecture, and culture must look like if space has only two dimensions — for example, no passing someone on the street without one of you climbing over the other.

## Relevance to Worldbuilding

A clean example of [[Nature and Invented Physics|natural invention]]: one constraint (two spatial dimensions) drives the whole world's locomotion, doors, digestion, and cities. It also fits [[Wolf's Taxonomy of Invention|ontological invention]].
