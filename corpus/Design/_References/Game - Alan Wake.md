**Developer:** Remedy Entertainment | **Year:** 2010 (Remastered 2021) | **Platform:** PC, console
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1622680/Alan_Wake_Remastered/)**

A third-person action-thriller set in a dark Pacific Northwest forest. The protagonist is a writer chased by shadowy enemies that can only be hurt after their darkness is burned away with a flashlight. Light is both the navigation tool and the weapon.
