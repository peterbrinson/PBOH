## What It Is
A sniper with one bullet per level — but the bullet can be re-aimed at the moment of each impact, so a whole compound of cultists becomes a single ballistic puzzle: plan the chain, fire once, steer the kill from head to head. Grimy, hallucinatory styling over a mechanic of total precision. The reference for one constrained verb (a single shot) generating an entire puzzle language.
