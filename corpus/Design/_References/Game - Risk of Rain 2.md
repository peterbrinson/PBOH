## What It Is
A third-person roguelike shooter. Your escape pod crashes onto a hostile alien planet, and you fight through a stage, find the teleporter, survive its charge, and move on to the next. The longer you take, the harder the enemies get — the clock is the main pressure. Items stack into absurd builds, and every death sends you back to the start.

## Meeting the World
Concerning [[Meeting the World]], this game jumps right in. The pod lands, the door opens, and you are already in the world as it will be throughout: the terrain, the enemies, the timer running, the teleporter somewhere out there. There's no quiet prologue that later gives way to "the real game" — the first minute *is* the game.
