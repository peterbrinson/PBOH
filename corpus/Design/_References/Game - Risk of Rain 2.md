## What It Is
A third-person roguelike shooter. Your escape pod crashes onto a hostile alien planet, and you fight. The longer you take, the harder the enemies get — time is the central pressure. Items stack into intricate builds, and every death sends you back to the start.

## Meeting the World
It is an interesting case of [[Meeting the World]].  ROR2 jumps right in. The pod lands, the door opens, and you are in the world and game loop as it will be throughout.
