**Designer:** Erhard Wissner | **Publisher:** Ravensburger | **Year:** 1988 | **Platform:** Tabletop

A family board game in which players race small boats up a river against a tide that pushes everything back. Its procedural rhetoric: boats are meant to reach a destination; persistence beats environment; luck, speed, and sacrifice are rewarded; it's better to be self-interested than spiteful; only one person can win.
