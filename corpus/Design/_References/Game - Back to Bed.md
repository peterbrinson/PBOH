## What It Is
An isometric puzzle game set inside the dreams of Bob, a chronic sleepwalker. The player controls Subob, Bob's subconscious guardian — a small dog-like figure — and must guide the oblivious sleepwalking Bob safely back to his bed by placing apples in his path to redirect him around hazards. The world is rendered in a Magritte- and Escher-inflected dreamscape: floating bowler hats, fish, doorways that wrap around the geometry of the level.
