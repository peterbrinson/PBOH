## What It Is
You are a shiba inu directing endless marching crowds of people — placing commands (turn, jump, float, climb) on the ground to route a river of humanity to salvation, Lemmings-style, across 90 stages. The crowds are both puzzle material and image: masses of identical figures pouring through brutalist white spaces, beautiful and unsettling at once. Mass behavior as mechanic *and* as metaphor.
