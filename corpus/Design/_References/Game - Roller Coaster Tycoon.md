**Developer:** Chris Sawyer | **Year:** 1999 (the version cited in lecture: 2003 platinum) | **Platform:** PC
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/285330/RollerCoaster_Tycoon_Deluxe/)**

A theme park management sim. New rides and upgrades take in-game time to research and build, and the game reveals a new animation or model when each one unlocks — wait time itself becomes a source of [[Anticipation in Games|anticipation]].
