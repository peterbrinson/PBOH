## What It Is
A faded movie star mounts a Broadway comeback, filmed to look like one single continuous shot — the camera prowls through backstage corridors, dressing rooms, rigging, and out onto the stage without ever appearing to cut. The labyrinth of the theatre becomes the inside of his unraveling head, and the unbroken movement means the audience *travels* the space rather than being shown it. A master reference for continuous presence through connected interiors.
