## What It Is
Two protagonists, two realities: an FBI agent investigates ritual murders in a Pacific Northwest town (crime-board deduction, evidence, place), while the writer Alan Wake is trapped in a Dark Place shaped like a nightmare New York — and can *rewrite scenes* of the world around him, swapping a location's reality from inside it. A horror story about authorship where the draft you're in keeps being revised. Touches all four roles at once — investigation, place and atmosphere, puzzle structure — but it lives deepest in the Dreamer: the world as an expression of a mind writing itself out of the dark.
