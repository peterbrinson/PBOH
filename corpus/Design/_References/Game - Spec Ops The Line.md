**Developer:** Yager | **Year:** 2012 | **Platform:** PC, console

A third-person military shooter set in post-disaster Dubai. Presents itself as a middle-of-the-road 2010s shooter; ends as a meditation on player complicity. The revelation reframes the player's relationship to the violence they've enacted — a touchstone for [[Revelation and Recontextualization]] and for games that interrogate their own genre.
