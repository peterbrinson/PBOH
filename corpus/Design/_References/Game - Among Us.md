**Developer:** Innersloth | **Year:** 2018 | **Platform:** PC, mobile, console
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/945360/Among_Us/)**

A multiplayer social deduction game. Crewmates complete tasks on a spaceship while impostors secretly sabotage and kill. Meetings are called to argue, accuse, and vote. The whole game runs on the tension between hidden information and group judgment.
