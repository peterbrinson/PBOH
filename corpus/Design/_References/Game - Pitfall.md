**Developer:** Activision (David Crane) | **Year:** 1982 | **Platform:** Atari 2600

A side-scrolling jungle adventure. Pitfall Harry runs through screens of scorpions, snakes, crocodiles, vines, and pits, collecting treasures against a 20-minute timer. Used in 389 as the in-class exercise for writing a [[The Macro|macro]] for a small classic game.
