## What It Is
A robot wakes in sunlit classical ruins where a sky-voice claiming to be God assigns puzzle after puzzle — lasers to route, drones to divert, time to replicate — while terminals scattered through the world needle you with philosophical questions about what you are and whether to obey. The puzzles teach mastery; the world teaches doubt — meaning lives in whether you climb the forbidden tower.

Its variation on keys-and-doors: a single kind of key opens three different kinds of doors. The purple portal visible in the first moments serves as doorway, landmark, and objective — a clean example of [[Reusing Objects and Spaces]].
