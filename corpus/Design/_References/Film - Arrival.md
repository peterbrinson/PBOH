## What It Is
A linguist is brought in to communicate with aliens whose written language has no beginning or end — and learning it begins to reshape how she experiences time. The plot is first contact; the achievement is *tone*: two hours of slow awe, dread, and grief held in perfect suspension, built from fog, low sound, and patience. A model for how atmosphere can carry meaning that exposition never states.
