## Relevance to Worldbuilding
Katabasis — the descent into the underworld — is one of the oldest worldbuilding moves in storytelling. Kuang's work engages this mythological structure directly, constructing an underworld with its own logic, rules, and inhabitants. A useful lens for thinking about how worlds built around death and passage function differently from worlds built around habitation.
