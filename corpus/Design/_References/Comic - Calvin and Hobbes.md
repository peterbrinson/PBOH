**Creator:** Bill Watterson | **Year:** 1985–1995 | **Format:** Newspaper comic strip

The newspaper comic following six-year-old Calvin and his stuffed tiger Hobbes. The "Spaceman Spiff" strips — Calvin's imagined fighter-pilot alter ego — are a recurring reference for how graphic design alone (size, bold color, exaggeration, composition, contrast between fantastical and mundane) can evoke a strong emotional reaction.
