## What It Is
A procedurally generated pastel island with no goals, no text, and no hands — just walking and listening. Every plant, animal, and weather pattern emits its own musical signature, so the soundtrack *is* your position in the world: chase frogs and the music hops, stand in rain and it shimmers. A season passes when you let it. The purest available example of presence and witnessing as the entire design.
