**Designer:** Lizzie Magie / Parker Brothers | **Year:** 1935 (current form) | **Platform:** Tabletop

The canonical American board game. Its procedural rhetoric: get as much money as you can; ownership and investment beat working; collect rent (good) instead of paying rent (bad); luck and persistence are rewarded; if you're wealthy, jail is just an inconvenience. Originally designed as a critique of land monopoly; the irony is that nearly no one notices.
