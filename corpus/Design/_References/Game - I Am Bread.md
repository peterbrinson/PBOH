## What It Is
You are a slice of bread, and your goal is to become toast. Each corner of the slice grips with a separate input, so movement is a deliberate, hilarious struggle — flopping end over end across kitchen counters toward anything hot enough, while staying edible. Comic physics embodiment: the entire game is the gap between a simple intention and an unruly body.
