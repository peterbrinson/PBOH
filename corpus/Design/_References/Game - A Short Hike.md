## What It Is
A bird named Claire hikes, climbs, and glides up Hawk Peak Provincial Park to find cell reception at the summit. Golden feathers extend your stamina, but nothing rushes you — you can fish, chat with other hikers, race a fox, or just wander the backcountry. Warm, small, and complete in ninety minutes: the reference for a single landscape that makes ascent feel like leisure instead of challenge.
