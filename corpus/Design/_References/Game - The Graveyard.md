## What It Is
You play an elderly woman who walks — slowly, with a cane — down the central path of a graveyard, sits on a bench, and listens to a song. That's the whole game; the developers call it "an explorable painting." In the full version, each play carries a chance she dies of natural causes on the bench. From the makers of The Path: presence, mortality, and a single short walk asked to carry everything.
