## What It Is
An idyllic 1950s company town — perfect houses, perfect dinners, husbands who drive into the desert each morning to a project no one questions — that begins to crack at the seams for one housewife. The utopia is a constructed reality, and the film's tension lives in its surfaces: the more flawless the world looks, the more wrong it feels. A clean example of an environment whose *perfection* is the threat.
