## What It Is

A first-person puzzle game set in the ruined Aperture Science testing facility. Portal 2 expands the original game's portal mechanic with new characters, surfaces, gels, and machinery, while the facility itself changes around the player.

## Expectation and Payoff

The opening begins with a tiny domestic routine. An automated voice asks the player to look around and return to bed, establishing the room as a stable place and the instructions as the game's apparent rhythm.

When Wheatley arrives, that same room is torn from its setting and carried through a collapsing facility. The payoff grows directly from the setup: an ordinary room becomes a vehicle, its walls become a thin disguise, and the scale and condition of the world are revealed through its destruction.
