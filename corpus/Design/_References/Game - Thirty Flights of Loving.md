## What It Is
A fifteen-minute first-person story about a heist gone wrong, told with *jump cuts* — the game hard-cuts you between moments mid-stride, out of order, and trusts you to assemble the story from fragments: a wedding, an airport, an orange peeled in slow motion. The first game to seriously import film editing into first-person space. The reference for non-linear storytelling through abrupt, confident scene transitions.
