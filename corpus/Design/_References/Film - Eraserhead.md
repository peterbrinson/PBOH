## What It Is
A man drifts through a desolate industrial landscape — hissing radiators, droning machinery, corridors of stained concrete — caring for a deformed child in a one-room apartment. Almost nothing is explained and almost nothing happens; the film is carried entirely by its environment and sound design, a sustained atmosphere of industrial dread you inhabit rather than follow. The reference for a world whose *texture* is the whole experience.
