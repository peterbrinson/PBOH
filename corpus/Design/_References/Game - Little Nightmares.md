## What It Is
A tiny child in a yellow raincoat sneaks, climbs, and hides through the Maw — a vast iron vessel of grotesque adults rendered at dollhouse scale, where furniture is terrain and a kitchen is a level. Physical puzzles and stealth inside a strictly bounded world, with horror built from scale: everything ordinary is enormous, and being small *is* the mechanic.
