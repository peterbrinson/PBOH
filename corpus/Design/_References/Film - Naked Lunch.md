## What It Is
An exterminator-turned-writer descends into a hallucinated city where his typewriter is a talking insect, reports are filed to handlers who may not exist, and the writing of the book *is* the drug experience. Addiction, guilt, and authorship blur into one continuous hallucination — the film never marks where reality ends. A reference for worlds where the protagonist's creative act and their delusion are the same thing.
