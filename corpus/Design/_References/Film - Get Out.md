**Director:** Jordan Peele | **Year:** 2017 | **Runtime:** 104 min

A horror-thriller about a Black photographer visiting his white girlfriend's family. Chris's small choices — to stay civil, to maintain composure under accumulating pressure — are dilemmas with double impact: they make us feel, *and* they're culturally insightful. A central example for [[Character Dilemmas]].
