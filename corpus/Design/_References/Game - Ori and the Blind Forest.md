**Developer:** Moon Studios | **Year:** 2015 | **Platform:** PC, console
**→ [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/261570/Ori_and_the_Blind_Forest/)**

A 2D metroidvania built around movement abilities. The double-jump is a classic "key that is an ability" — gaining it transforms which parts of the map you can reach without any new object having entered the world.
