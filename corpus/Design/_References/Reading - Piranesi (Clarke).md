**Author:** Susanna Clarke · **Year:** 2020 · **Type:** Novel
→ [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Piranesi-Susanna-Clarke/dp/1635577802)

A man known as Piranesi lives in the House: an endless labyrinth of grand halls, their lower floors flooded by tides, their upper halls filled with clouds, every wall lined with statues. He believes the House is the entire world and keeps meticulous journals of its tides and rooms. The novel slowly reveals that the House connects, through hidden ways, to our own world — and how he came to be there.

## Relevance to Worldbuilding

Piranesi is a near-perfect [[Bounded Worlds|Bounded World]]: the House is a bounded, self-consistent labyrinth the narrator treats as complete in itself, yet it connects to our world through hidden passages. Clarke withholds far more than she explains, making the House a sustained [[Catalysts of Speculation|catalyst of speculation]] — meaning accumulates through the narrator's patient, committed observation of statues, tides, and halls. It is a vivid case for [[Meeting the World|how a player meets a world]].
