**Developer:** Adam Cadre | **Year:** 1998 | **Platform:** Web (Inform 6)
**→ [Play online](https://adamcadre.ac/if/photopia.html)**

A parser-based interactive fiction work. Its color-coded sections (red, white, blue, gold, sky blue, purple) tell a nonlinear story whose pieces only resolve as you piece together how the scenes connect. A canonical IF reference for what the medium can do at its most controlled.
