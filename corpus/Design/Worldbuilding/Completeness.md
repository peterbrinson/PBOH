Mark Wolf (p.38–39): completeness is a **degree**.  Every world has gaps. The question is how those gaps are handled.

In worldbuilding, unnecessary details might help believability, unlike traditional storytelling (where every detail earns its place by advancing the plot). In worldbuilding a detail that goes nowhere narratively can still make the place feel inhabited.
