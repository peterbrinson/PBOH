What are you focusing on, as a designer, when you craft your labyrinth?
Do you want the labyrinth's situations to primarily express meaning, or instead provide the player comprelling choices?
Are they immersed like in most 3D mazes, or looking down, seeing a larger section of the world.

Despite these qualities being set up on linear axes, none of the 4 principles are mutually exclusive.
