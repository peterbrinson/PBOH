Every worldbuilding endeavor begins somewhere. Three common entry points:

- **Story-first** — you have a narrative and build the world to support it
- **Systems-first** — you have rules or mechanics and discover the world they imply
- **World-first** — you have a place, and narrative or systems emerge from inhabiting it

Or framed by intent:
- **Polemic** — the world is an argument
- **Theme** — the world is an exploration of an idea
- **Experience** — the world is something you want the player to feel

## Situational Game Design

[[Reading - Situational Game Design (Upton)|Situational Game Design]] is a useful influence here, particularly on student-scale projects — the idea that games are designed around a specific player situation.
