There are a few ways to think about worlds with boundaries.
Some finite worlds feel complete in themselves. Some present edges that can be approached but not passed. Others are structured around boundaries the player can cross.

## Three Structures: How a World Defines Its Edges

### 1. A World Without an Outside

The world presents itself as whole. The player engages entirely within what is present — the world is the totality.

- [[Film - Balance (1989)|Balance]] (1989) — the world is the platform and the five characters; nothing else exists
- [[Game - Monument Valley|Monument Valley]] (2014) a set of micro-worlds, each small and finite
- [[Game - Mountain|Mountain]] (2014) — you are a mountain
- [[Game - Inscryption|Inscryption]] (2021) — Act 1: the table, the cabin, the game
- [[Game - 12 Minutes|12 Minutes]] (2021) — three rooms, one looping conversation; the world is the apartment
- [[Game - Thomas Was Alone|Thomas Was Alone]] (2012)
- [Journey](https://store.steampowered.com/app/638230/Journey/) (2012)
- [Tetris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetris) (1984)
- [Aesop's Fables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aesop%27s_Fables)

A transition from 1 to 2:  [[Book - Yertle the Turtle|Yertle the Turtle]] (1958) — expanding boundary

### 2. A World With an Uncrossable Boundary

The player can encounter the edge of the world. The boundary is felt during play — the system holds the player within its limits — but cannot be passed.

- [The Exterminating Angel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exterminating_Angel) (Luis Buñuel, 1962) - bourgeois characters are stuck in their own world
- [[Game - Dr Langeskov|Dr Langeskov, The Tiger, and The Terribly Cursed Emerald: A Whirlwind Heist]] - backstage from the real (and inaccessible) game
- [[Film - Snowpiercer|Snowpiercer]] (Bong Joon-ho, 2013)
- [Flatland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatland) (Edwin Abbott, 1884)
- [[Film - The Platform|The Platform]] (Galder Gaztelu-Urrutia, 2019)
- [[Game - PT|P.T.]] (Kojima Productions, 2014)
- [Slaughterhouse-Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slaughterhouse-Five) (Kurt Vonnegut, 1969) — Billy Pilgrim is kept in an alien zoo; 
- Escape rooms
- Terrariums, zoos, prisons

### 3. A World With a Crossable Boundary

The player can approach and pass from one state or space into another. 

- [[Game - Portal|Portal]] (2007)
- [[Game - Psychonauts|Psychonauts]] (2005)
- [[Film - World of Tomorrow|World of Tomorrow]] (Don Hertzfeldt, 2015)
- [[Film - Spirited Away|Spirited Away]] (Miyazaki, 2001)
- [[Reading - Piranesi (Clarke)|Piranesi]] (Susanna Clarke, 2020)
- [Alice in Wonderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice%27s_Adventures_in_Wonderland) (Lewis Carroll, 1865)
- [[Film - The Matrix|The Matrix]] (Wachowski Sisters, 1999)
- [[Film - Inception|Inception]] (Christopher Nolan, 2010)
- [[Film - Paprika|Paprika]] (Satoshi Kon, 2006)
- [[Film - Pan's Labyrinth|Pan's Labyrinth]] (Guillermo del Toro, 2006)
- [[Film - Twin Peaks|Twin Peaks]] (1990–1991, 2017)
- [[Film - Severance|Severance]] (2022–)
- [[Game - Hades|Hades]] (2020)
- [The Chronicles of Narnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia) (C.S. Lewis, 1950–1956)
