### PBOH — Peter B's Office Hours
PBOH is a queryable knowledge bundle — course material coupled with the instructions that turn an LLM into your guide.  
It provides a starting point for a student crafting a game from a storytelling or worldbuilding framework.   Additionally, PBOH gives concrete advice when working with the Unreal Game engine, but PBOH is useful when working with any engine.

---

- **[[Development/Tutorials - LLM|Set up your AI tool]]** — pick a quick start, get PBOH running, and have your first conversation.
- **[[How It Works]]** — what turns a folder of text into a guide.

---

PBOH embraces the qualities Google's [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md) recently articulated.

The website you're reading now is a browsable copy of that material; the repository — [github.com/peterbrinson/PBOH](https://github.com/peterbrinson/PBOH) — is the proper version, features and all.
