---

1. **Get the repository.** Clone or fork [GDOH on GitHub](https://github.com/gdofficehours/GDOH).
2. **Set up an AI tool.** The [[Development/Tutorials - LLM|Tutorials - LLM]] tutorials walk through installing and setting up the major AI coding agents.
3. **Open the repository with that agent** and start a session — it reads GDOH and becomes your guide.
