## What It Is
A mother and her two light-sensitive children keep a strict ritual of locked doors and drawn curtains in a fog-bound manor, as unseen intruders seem to move through the rooms. The famous reveal inverts the haunting — recoloring every scene that came before, every noise, every locked door. Underneath the twist: two households occupying the *same space* without perceiving each other, each experiencing the other as the ghost.
