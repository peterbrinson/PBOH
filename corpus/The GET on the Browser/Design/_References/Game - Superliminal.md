## What It Is
A first-person puzzle game played inside a dream. The central mechanic is forced perspective: pick up an object, move it closer or farther from the camera, and it physically resizes to match its apparent size. A chess piece held against a doorway becomes a chess piece tall enough to climb. Framed as a sleep-therapy program gone strange.
