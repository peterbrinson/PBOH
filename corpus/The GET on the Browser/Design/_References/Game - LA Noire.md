## What It Is
A detective procedural in meticulously rebuilt 1947 Los Angeles: walk crime scenes for physical evidence, then sit suspects down and read their faces — the era-defining facial capture exists so you can judge, line by line, whether a witness is lying. Cases climb from traffic desk to homicide as the city's postwar glamour rots underneath. The big-budget reference for evidence-gathering and interrogation as core verbs.
