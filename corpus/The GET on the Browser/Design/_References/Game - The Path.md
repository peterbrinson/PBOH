## What It Is
A retelling of Little Red Riding Hood. You play six sisters in turn; each is told to walk to grandmother's house and stay on the path. The actual game is what happens when you leave it — each sister meets her own wolf in the forest, a figure shaped to her particular age and identity. The canonical Traveler game: a clear instruction the world is built for you to reconsider, where straying *is* the meaning.
