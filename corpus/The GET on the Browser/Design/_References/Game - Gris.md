## What It Is
A wordless platformer about grief, played as a young woman moving through a watercolor world that has lost its color. Each stage of the journey returns one color and one ability — her dress hardens to stone, becomes a glider — and each new ability literally re-colors and reopens the world. No death, no failure, no danger: the entire design question is how to make an emotional progression *playable*.
