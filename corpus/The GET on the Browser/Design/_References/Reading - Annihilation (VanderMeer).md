Novel by Jeff VanderMeer (2014). First book of the Southern Reach trilogy. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Annihilation-Novel-Southern-Reach-Trilogy/dp/0374104093)

## Relevance to Worldbuilding
Four unnamed women enter Area X — a coastal zone sealed off from the world for decades, where nature has reclaimed all human traces. The rules of identity and biology don't hold inside. A biologist's husband entered on a previous expedition and returned, but wrong.

Area X is one of the most effective examples of [[Wolf's Taxonomy of Invention|ontological invention]] in contemporary fiction: the world doesn't just have different creatures or cultures, it operates under different fundamental rules. The reader never gets a clean explanation. The world resists comprehension by design.

There is also a 2018 film adaptation by Alex Garland — see [[Film - Annihilation]].
