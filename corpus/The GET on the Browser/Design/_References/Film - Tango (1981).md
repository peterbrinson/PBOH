## What It Is
A single static shot of one cramped room. A ball bounces in; a boy climbs through the window to fetch it — and his loop repeats. Then another person enters on their own loop, then another, until thirty-six characters from every stage of life are layered into the same space, each repeating their own thread, never colliding. Parallel lives as choreography: one room holding dozens of simultaneous, independent stories.
